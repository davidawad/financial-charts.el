# 3D financial charts (graphics.el)

Generated by `make golden-3d` (`financial-chart-3d-write-gallery`); do not
edit by hand.

Every financial-chart template has a 3D variant in `templates3d/`: the
template with its body unchanged plus an `x-eas.graphics` naming a
[graphics.el](https://gitlab.com/davidawad/graphics-el) mark
(graphics.el's `docs/design/eas-integration.md`).
`src/financial-chart-3d.el` registers them with eas under the namespace
`fc3d`, so `fc3d/ohlc` is the variant of `ohlc`. eas still draws each in
2D; graphics.el draws it in 3D.

graphics.el is optional: without it everything 2D works as before and the
3D commands signal `financial-chart-3d-unavailable`, saying how to install
it.

```elisp
(require 'financial-chart-3d)
(financial-chart-3d-open "ohlc" :show t)                 ; its example
(financial-chart-3d-open "ohlc" :bindings '(:bars BARS) :show t)
M-x financial-chart-3d-gallery     ; every variant, a grid of windows
M-x financial-chart-3d-demo-book   ; the order-book surface, streaming
(setq v (financial-chart-3d-book-open SNAPSHOT :show t))  ; live book
(financial-chart-3d-book-push v DELTAS)
```

In a 3D buffer the arrows orbit, `+`/`-` zoom and `0` resets; a replay
plays and pauses with `SPC`, steps with `,` and `.` and changes speed
with `[` and `]`.

Marks: `bar3d` and `depth3d` are graphics.el's own. The `fc-` marks are
registered by `financial-chart-3d.el` with `graphics-eas-define-mark`:
candles whose scales span the whole series, stacked panes, a volume
profile beside candles, the underwater curve, a payoff heightfield, depth
walls and sign-coloured bars.

A replay draws every row at every moment: a series starts from its first
third in colour, the rest a dim ghost the replay colours bar by bar; a
payoff surface stays whole while a bright trace sweeps it to expiry. The
camera is fitted to the data (`financial-chart-3d-camera-fit`), so it
fills its pane at any size and holds still while a replay plays.

Each render below is the variant's golden (`test/golden/3d/NAME.txt`): its
example at 60x24 cells, dark palette, the fitted camera, at the end of its
replay, drawn by the Rust core; a replay's middle follows it
(`test/golden/3d/t0.5/NAME.txt`). The Braille text keeps the dots, not the
colours. `make test-3d` checks every variant against its template, draws
its goldens bit for bit and checks it reads at five sizes and five phases
(`test/financial-chart-3d-framing-test.el`).

| variant | mark | replay | 3D reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| [depth-live](#depth-live) | `depth3d` |  | order-book surface: cumulative depth by price (across) and step (back), streaming live from financial-chart-3d-book-push |
| [depth](#depth) | `fc-depth3d` |  | cumulative depth as two walls stepping away from the mid, bids in front, asks behind |
| [diverging-bars](#diverging-bars) | `fc-diverging3d` |  | one 3D bar per label, up from the floor for gains and down through it for losses |
| [drawdown](#drawdown) | `fc-drawdown3d` | yes | the underwater curve as a slab hanging below the high-water line, seen from below; replays from its first third point by point |
| [ladder](#ladder) | `bar3d` |  | the price ladder as 3D bars, one per level, bids and asks in two lanes; live pushes grow and shrink them |
| [ohlc](#ohlc) | `fc-candle3d` | yes | candles as boxes along time, price up, each body as deep as its volume; replays from its first third bar by bar |
| [panes](#panes) | `fc-panes3d` | yes | price, volume and RSI(14) as three stacked panels on one time axis; replays from its first third bar by bar |
| [payoff-curves](#payoff-curves) | `fc-payoff3d` | yes | the curves as one P/L surface over price and curve (a strategy at several dates: a surface toward expiry); a bright trace sweeps it curve by curve |
| [payoff](#payoff) | `fc-payoff3d` | yes | P/L surface over price and days to expiry, the earlier days priced from the expiry payoff; a bright trace sweeps it to expiry |
| [volume-profile](#volume-profile) | `fc-volume-profile3d` | yes | horizontal volume-at-price bars beside the candles, point of control bright; the profile builds as the bars replay from their first third |
